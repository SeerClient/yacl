- Never lock the player/level/entity Mutexes. Use `game::with_player`, `game::level()`,
  `game::with_entity`, `game::with_chest_menu`. Inside a mixin handler whose target is a
  player/entity method, `this` comes from the hook (`ci`), never from `with_player`.
